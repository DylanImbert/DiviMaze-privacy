Privacy policy – DiviMaze

Last updated: November 18, 2026

DiviMaze is a puzzle game developed by Meteor Tales (independent developer). This policy explains what data is processed when you use the Android app.

1. Data stored on your device

Your progress (completed levels, hints, missions, language and sound settings) is stored locally on your device. It is not sent to our servers.

2. Google Play Games cloud save

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your progress may be saved to Google Play Games cloud storage so you can restore it on another device. This is handled by Google under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can erase this save at any time from the game settings ("Reset progress").

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Before the first ad, a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area). Depending on your choice, ads may be personalised or not.

AdMob may collect an advertising identifier and technical information. See: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)

The game uses Firebase Analytics to measure usage in aggregate: level start and end, hint usage, ads shown, missions completed. No data is sent until you accept the consent in the form above. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.

5. In-app purchases (Google Play)

DiviMaze offers a one-time purchase that removes ads. The purchase is processed by Google Play, which handles payment details; we do not receive or store them. The purchase is linked to your Google Play Games sign-in and is restored automatically when you sign in again. Refund requests are made through Google Play under its own policy.

6. Data we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts, location, or photos.

7. Children

DiviMaze is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Your rights

You can withdraw your advertising and statistics consent at any time via the consent form or your device settings. Uninstalling the app deletes local data. For questions: contact.meteortales@gmail.com.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
